# CiteOrAbstain - Comprehensive Literature Review Matrix (10 Core Papers)

This document provides an exhaustive, 11-column literature review matrix covering 10 foundational peer-reviewed studies directly guiding the architectural design, safety rules, and evaluation methodology of the **CiteOrAbstain** knee replacement patient question-answering assistant.

| **Dense Passage Retrieval for Open-Domain Question Answering (DPR)** *(Karpukhin et al.)* | 2020 | Traditional keyword retrieval (BM25) fails to capture semantic synonyms and natural colloquial patient phrasing. | Trains dual-encoder architectures using dense vectors, mapping questions and passages into a shared latent semantic space. | Natural Questions, TriviaQA, WebQuestions, CuratedTREC. | Dual BERT-base encoders. | Dense Passage Retrieval (DPR) indexed via FAISS. | Top-k Retrieval Accuracy (Top-20, Top-100 hit rates). | Substantially outperforms BM25 on top-k retrieval accuracy by capturing semantic intent rather than exact keyword overlap. | Can miss highly specific, rare medical technical codes where exact token matching is necessary. | Justifies our architectural choice of sentence-transformer embeddings in ChromaDB to match patient queries with clinical guides. |

## Key Methodological Takeaways for CiteOrAbstain

1. **Grounded Generation Mandate:** Following Lewis et al. and Gao et al., answers must be generated exclusively from retrieved passages, never from unconstrained parametric memory.
2. **Two-Stage Abstention Pipeline:**
   - **Stage 1 (Rule-Based Safety):** Immediate refusal and clinical redirection for medication dose adjustments and acute emergencies (aligned with Med-PaLM clinical safety standards).
   - **Stage 2 (Threshold-Based Abstention):** Fallback refusal (*"I don't know, please ask your care team"*) when vector retrieval confidence falls below the calibrated threshold (Yin et al., Zhang et al.).
3. **Multi-Faceted Evaluation Strategy:** As established by RAGAS, ALCE, and AttrEval, our evaluation script will report three strictly decoupled metrics: *Answer Correctness*, *Citation Precision*, and *Correct Refusal Rate*.
